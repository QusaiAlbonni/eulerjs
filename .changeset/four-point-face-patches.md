A face resting on another face (a box on a box, a hull on a hull) now keeps the four contact points that span the patch instead of up to eight, so stacks stand steadier (the top of an awake 8-box stack moves 0.4 mm in a minute, from 11.7 mm) and piles of hulls solve faster. Bodies resting across several triangles keep up to eight.
